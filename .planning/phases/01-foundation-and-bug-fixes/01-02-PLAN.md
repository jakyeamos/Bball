<objective>
Wire the two missing quarter-by-quarter coaching WebSocket events: SUBMIT_QUARTER_COACHING and READY_FOR_QUARTER. Purpose: The quarter coaching flow is defined but non-functional. A user who submits quarter decisions currently gets no response — the game hangs. This fix closes that gap.
Output: Two new handler functions in handlers-v2.ts, two new socket.on() registrations in socketManager.ts, and READY_FOR_QUARTER added to WS_EVENTS in shared/types.ts.
</objective>
