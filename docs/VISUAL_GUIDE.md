## Color Coding Explained

### Archetype Categories

#### 🔵 Offensive (Blue Tags)
Players who contribute primarily on offense:
- **Primary Creator** - Main ball handler, creates shots for self/others
- **Secondary Creator** - Can handle ball but not primary
- **Connector** - Good passer, keeps offense flowing
- **Off Ball Shooter** - Catch-and-shoot specialist
- **Movement Shooter** - Gets shots via screens/movement
- **Slasher** - Attacks rim, drives to basket
- **Post Scorer** - Back-to-basket scorer
- **Playmaking Big** - Big man who can pass

#### 🔴 Defensive (Red Tags)
Players who excel on defense:
- **POA Stopper** - Point of attack defender (guards ball handler)
- **Help Defender** - Weak-side help, team defense
- **Rim Protector** - Blocks shots at rim
- **Def Anchor** - Defensive quarterback, organizes defense
- **Def Playmaker** - Creates steals, deflections

#### 🟢 Hybrid (Green Tags)
Versatile players who do multiple things:
- **Three And D** - Shoots 3s + plays defense
- **Stretch Big** - Big man who shoots 3s
- **Vertical Roller** - Catch lobs, finish at rim
- **Rebounder** - Elite on the glass
- **Utility Wing** - Does a bit of everything

## How to Read Team Composition

### Ball Handlers (Creators)
**What it measures:** PrimaryCreator + SecondaryCreator archetypes

**Thresholds:**
- ✅ **0-30%** = Balanced, no penalty
- ⚠️ **30-40%** = Warning, approaching penalty
- 🚨 **40%+** = Penalty active, team struggles with too many ball-dominant players

**Why it matters:** Too many creators = ball sticks, spacing suffers

---

### Shooting/Spacing
**What it measures:** OffBallShooter + MovementShooter archetypes

**Thresholds:**
- ✅ **25%+** = Excellent, floor is spaced
- ⚠️ **15-25%** = Decent, adequate spacing
- 🚨 **<15%** = Poor, defenses pack the paint

**Why it matters:** Shooting creates space for everyone else

---

### Rim Protection
**What it measures:** RimProtector + DefAnchor archetypes

**Thresholds:**
- ✅ **10%+** = Solid interior defense
- ⚠️ **5-10%** = Vulnerable, can be exploited
- 🚨 **<5%** = Major weakness, opposing teams attack rim easily

**Why it matters:** Need someone to protect the rim vs drives

## Technical Details

All calculations are **weighted by player impact rating**, so your best players matter most:

```
Team Creator % = Σ (player creator % × player weight)

where weight = player impact / total team impact
```

This means:
- Your windriver players (high impact) have more influence
- Bench players (low impact) matter less
- Reflects actual on-court importance
